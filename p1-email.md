**Presentation Format**

Each presenter gets ~9.5 minutes. Your TA will keep time.

- **4 min** — Present your deck as if to a client
- **3 min** — Q&A (pod members play the role of client stakeholders)
- **2 min** — Everyone completes written feedback
- **30 sec** — Transition to next presenter

**Your Deck (4-6 main slides, appendix okay)**

1. **The Company at a Glance** — What they do, where they play, recent performance
2. **What I Noticed** — One non-obvious pattern, trend, or tension from your outside-in research
3. **Supporting Evidence** — Data that backs up your observation (financials, benchmarks, industry trends)
4. **Why This Matters** — What's at stake if they act or don't?

**Feedback**

After each presenter, everyone completes the feedback form: [Presentation Feedback Form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSccVuQm1vhbx2-6-AC6vDEKqXnkLnaVVIY3RAzCN7JWk6oWMw/viewform?usp=header)

You are rating on three dimensions:

- **Informed (40%)** — Do they understand the company's world?
- **Compelling (40%)** — Would you want to explore this further?
- **Credible (20%)** — Would you trust this person with a real project?

Plus one written strength and one thing to work on.

**You must submit feedback for every pod member. Missing any form = 0 feedback points (15 pts).**
