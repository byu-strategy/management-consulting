**Your Deck (5-8 slides, appendix okay)**

Building on your [P1 Intelligence Brief](https://byu-strategy.github.io/management-consulting/00-assessments.html#intelligence-brief), you're now taking a position: "Here's an opportunity I see, and here's what I believe you should do about it." See the full [P2: Point of View instructions](https://byu-strategy.github.io/management-consulting/00-assessments.html#point-of-view).

1. **The Opportunity** — One sentence: "I believe [Company] should [do X] because [Y]"
2. **Why This, Why Now** — What's changed or what makes this urgent? External pressures, competitive moves, market timing
3. **What It Would Take** — High-level approach: how would you structure the work? 2-3 key questions to answer
4. **What's at Stake** — Quantified potential impact. What happens if they act vs. don't act?
5. **What I'd Want to Discuss** — The 2-3 questions you'd want to explore in a conversation

**Feedback**

After each presenter, everyone completes the feedback form: [Presentation Feedback Form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSccVuQm1vhbx2-6-AC6vDEKqXnkLnaVVIY3RAzCN7JWk6oWMw/viewform?usp=header)

You are rating on three dimensions:

- **Informed (40%)** — Do they understand the company's world?
- **Compelling (40%)** — Would you want to explore this further?
- **Credible (20%)** — Would you trust this person with a real project?

Plus one written strength and one thing to work on.
