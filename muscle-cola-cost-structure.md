# Muscle Cola: De Novo Startup Cost Structure Analysis

*Scenario: Protein-infused cola startup, pre-2018 market entry*

---

## Target Population / Launch Market

A standard de novo beverage launch targets a **single metro area (MSA) of 1--3 million people**. This lets you:

- Prove unit economics before scaling
- Build retailer velocity data (cases/store/week)
- Manage distribution logistics with one warehouse
- Concentrate marketing spend for awareness

Good market analogs: Salt Lake City (~1.2M metro), Austin (~2M), or Nashville (~1.9M). Target placement in ~200--500 retail doors (gyms, convenience stores, independent grocers, supplement shops) within that MSA.

---

## Variable Costs (Per 12 oz Aluminum Can)

Assumes co-packed production (no owned facility), ~20g protein per serving via whey protein isolate, and a minimum initial run of ~5,000--10,000 cases (24-packs).

| Cost Component                                | Est. Cost/Unit | Notes                                  |
| --------------------------------------------- | -------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Whey protein isolate (~22g WPI per can)        | $0.15--0.20    | Bulk WPI at ~$3--4/lb in 2018          |
| Cola base ingredients (sweetener, flavoring, carbonation, water) | $0.08--0.12 | Standard CSD ingredient costs |
| Aluminum can + lid                             | $0.06--0.10    | Standard 12 oz sleek can               |
| Label / shrink sleeve                          | $0.02--0.04    | Printed sleeve at startup volumes      |
| Co-packing / filling fee                       | $0.10--0.18    | Batching, carbonation, filling, QC     |
| Case packaging (24-pack tray/wrap)             | $0.02--0.03    | Corrugate + shrink per unit            |
| Freight to warehouse                           | $0.05--0.10    | Depends on co-packer distance          |
| **Total variable cost per can**                | **$0.48--0.77**| |

### Pricing and Margin Structure

At a suggested retail price (SRP) of **$2.49--$2.99/can** and wholesale price to retailers of ~$1.50--1.80/can:

| Metric                        | Estimate       |
| ----------------------------- | -------------- |
| Wholesale price to retailer   | ~$1.50--1.80   |
| Variable COGS per can         | ~$0.48--0.77   |
| **Gross contribution per can**| **~$0.73--1.32** |
| **Gross margin (on wholesale)** | **~45--73%** |

The wide range reflects volume sensitivity --- at startup volumes (5,000 cases), you're at the high end of COGS; at 50,000+ cases, you'd approach the low end.

## Summary Unit Economics

For a midpoint scenario (~20,000 cases / ~480,000 cans in Year 1, serving a ~1.5M population MSA):

| Item                          | Value                          |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Revenue (wholesale)           | ~$780,000                      |
| Variable costs                | ~$300,000                      |
| Gross contribution            | ~$480,000                      |
| Fixed costs                   | ~$450,000                      |
| **Net contribution (Year 1)** | **~$30,000** (near breakeven)  |

---

## Key Technical Risk

The key challenge for a protein cola specifically is **protein stability in carbonation** --- whey protein tends to foam and denature in carbonated solutions, which drives up formulation costs and may require specialized emulsifiers or alternative protein sources (collagen peptides were emerging for this reason around 2018).
